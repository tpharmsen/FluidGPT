Motivation

The problem

Motivation

High-fidelity CFD is accurate — and expensive

  • Fine meshes and small time steps are required for stable, accurate solutions
  • Each design iteration re-runs the full solver — no reuse across similar geometries
  • This turns design exploration into the bottleneck, not the physics

Method

The approach

Method

Solver vs. surrogate

Classical CFD

Discretizes Navier–Stokes on a mesh

Iterates to convergence, every run from scratch

Cost scales with mesh resolution

Neural surrogate

Learns the mapping from geometry + BCs to flow field

Single forward pass, no iteration at inference

Cost is fixed, independent of mesh resolution

Architecture

Encoder → latent field → decoder

Governing physics

Physics-informed training objective

∂u/∂t + (u·∇)u = −(1/ρ)∇p + ν∇²u
The loss combines a supervised data term against RANS ground truth with a residual penalty on this momentum equation, keeping predictions physically consistent between training samples.
