Training & Validation

Spectral Analysis & Corresponding Metric

  • Pointwise error can't capture whether diverging predictions still reproduce the underlying physics of turbulent flows
  • Assesses whether models correctly reproduce the scale distribution of energy & enstrophy across wavenumber \(k\)
  • Each trajectory has its own ground-truth reference spectrum at time \(t\) → need for aggregation metric
  • Applied to energy spectrum \(E_n(k)\) and enstrophy spectrum \(Z_n(k)\)
  • Flowmatching Sampling
    • 16 rollout samples per input
    • 20-step Euler integration of velocity field over flow-time \(t_f\)
  • Excludes pdebench-comp due to insufficient data
  • At each time \(t\), for trajectory \(n\) and sample \(m\):
  • \( R_{n,m}(k) = \dfrac{E^{\mathrm{pred}}_{n,m}(k)}{E^{\mathrm{ref}}_n(k)} \)
  • \( w_n(k) = \dfrac{E^{\mathrm{ref}}_n(k)}{\sum_{j=1}^{N} E^{\mathrm{ref}}_j(k)} \)
  • \( \bar{\epsilon}(k) = \dfrac{1}{N M} \sum_{n=1}^{N} \sum_{m=1}^{M} w_n(k)\left|R_{n,m}(k)-1\right| \)
