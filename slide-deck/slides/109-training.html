Validation Methodology

Spectral Diagnostics

  • Motivation
    • Pointwise error alone can't capture physical fidelity in turbulent flows
    • Assesses energy & enstrophy cascade across scales (wavenumber k)
    • Excludes pdebench-comp (compressible, lacks incompressibility/pressure info)
  • Weighted Spectral Error Ratio
    • Spectral ratio: Rn,m(k) = Epredn,m(k) / Erefn(k)
    • Energy weighting: wn(k) = Erefn(k) / ∑ⱼ Erefj(k)
    • Weighted error: εn,m(k) = wn(k) · |Rn,m(k) − 1|
    • Generalized error: ε̄(k) = (1/M) ∑ₙ ∑ₘ εn,m(k)
  • Applied to
    • Kinetic energy spectrum En(k)
    • Enstrophy spectrum Zn(k) (same procedure, E → Z)
