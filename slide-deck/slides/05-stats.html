Traditional Numerical Solvers: Bound by the CFL stability condition, requiring iterative, sequential time-stepping over dense meshes to solve the Navier-Stokes equations.

Neural Operator Surrogates: Bypass iterative steps via a single, fully parallelized GPU forward pass, unlocking a ~15,000x theoretical speedup for real-time engineering design cycles.
