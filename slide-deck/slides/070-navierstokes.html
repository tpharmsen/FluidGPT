Problem Formulation · Navier Stokes

The Navier-Stokes PDEs

1. Mass Conservation (Continuity)

$$ \nabla \cdot u = 0 $$

Enforces the incompressibility constraint, ensuring the velocity field divergence remains zero across the domain.

2. Momentum Conservation

$$ \frac{\partial u}{\partial t} + (u \cdot \nabla)u = -\frac{1}{\rho}\nabla p + \nu\nabla^2 u $$

Governs velocity field dynamics by balancing unsteady acceleration and advection against pressure forces and viscous diffusion.
