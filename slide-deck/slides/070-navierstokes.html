Problem Formulation · Navier-Stokes

The Compressible Navier-Stokes PDEs

1. Mass Conservation (Continuity)

$$ \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\rho u) = 0 $$

Relates changes in density in time to the in- or outflow of mass.

2. Momentum Conservation

$$ \frac{\partial u}{\partial t} + (u \cdot \nabla)u = -\frac{1}{\rho}\nabla p + \frac{1}{\rho}\nabla \cdot \tau + f $$

Governs the evolution of the velocity field through advection, pressure gradients, viscous stresses, and forcing.
