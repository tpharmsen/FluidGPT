Energy & Enstrophy

Kinetic Energy

$$ E(t) = \frac{1}{2}\int_{\Omega} |u(x,t)|^2 \, dx $$

Total kinetic energy contained in the velocity field over the domain $\Omega$; tracks how much motion the flow retains as it evolves.

Enstrophy

$$ Z(t) = \frac{1}{2}\int_{\Omega} |\omega(x,t)|^2 \, dx, \quad \omega = \nabla \times u $$

Integrated squared vorticity, measuring the intensity of rotation and small-scale structure; a key indicator of turbulent dissipation.
