Overview

Contributions

01
A custom efficient spatiotemporal backbone
A hierarchical Swin Transformer backbone extended with custom space-then-time attention blocks and a UNet-style encoder-decoder structure, adapted for multi-dataset pretraining on 2D Navier-Stokes velocity fields.
02
A probabilistic flow-matching surrogate model
We take a dual modelling approach where a conditional flow-matching model learns a full distribution over future fluid states, rather than a single point estimate. Flow-matching outperforms deterministic baselines on the large majority of evaluated datasets.
03
A large-scale multi-source pretraining dataset
Nine heterogeneous datasets curated, preprocessed, and unified into a common training pipeline, spanning a broad spectrum of Navier-Stokes regimes to support generalisation across flow conditions.
04
Systematic evaluation across accuracy, stability, and spectral fidelity
All models are evaluated under single-step prediction, auto-regressive rollout, and spectral diagnostic coherence, providing a structured comparison of deterministic and probabilistic modelling strategies.
