Results

Conclusions

  • Limitations
    • Prior definition a fixed, non-adapted prior limits performance on high-frequency, sharply-varying fields
    • Unobserved forcing no model receives external body-forcing information, capping rollout accuracy where such forcing is active
    • Comparability of models shared architecture aids fairness, but each formulation has its own tuning space, leaving part of the gap unexplained
    • Compressible spectral diagnostics velocity-only predictions cannot yield energy or enstrophy spectra for the compressible dataset
  • Future work
    • data-dependent priors
    • forcing terms as symbolic input
    • hyperparameter tuning
    • discretized grid & obstructed flow & density input & pressure input
    • validation/fine-tuning to new flow regimes
