Energy & Enstrophy

Kinetic Energy

$$ E(t) = \frac{1}{2}\int_{\Omega} |u(x,t)|^2 \, dx $$

Describes the total kinetic energy in the system at each wavenumber

Enstrophy

$$ Z(t) = \frac{1}{2}\int_{\Omega} |\omega(x,t)|^2 \, dx, \quad \omega = \nabla \times u $$

Captures the distribution of rotational structure in the flow
