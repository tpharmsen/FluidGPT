Validation Methodology

Error Metric & Evaluation Protocol

  • Relative Absolute Error (RAE)
    • Primary pointwise, scale-invariant error metric
    • RAE = (1/N) ∑ᵢ |yᵢ − ŷᵢ| / |yᵢ|
    • Summed over all spatial & temporal dimensions of the predicted timeblock
    • Computed in float32 precision for accurate error measurement
  • Single-Step Validation
    • 5 ground-truth input timesteps → predict next timeblock (no feedback)
    • Isolates transition accuracy, avoids error accumulation
    • Metric: average per-sample error over next 5 predicted timesteps
  • Rollout Validation
    • Auto-regressive: predictions recursively fed back as inputs
    • Errors computed at every timestep of the rollout horizon
    • Includes non-natural initialization step → tests OOD robustness
  • Flow-Matching Sampling
    • 16 stochastic samples per input (8 for amira)
    • 20-step Euler integration of velocity field over flow-time tf
    • Errors aggregated across all drawn samples
