Dataset-specific Challenges

  • Wide variety in flow regimes
    • Compressible and Incompressible PDE form
    • Laminar and Turbulent flow
    • Different boundary conditions
    • With or without forcing terms
    • Different initialisation methods
    • Different viscosity, pressure and density parameters
  • Compatability of datasets
    • Preprocessing spatial and temporal resolutions
    • Preprocessing data formats
    • Uniform 2-d grid types
