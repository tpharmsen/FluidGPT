Custom Attention Block

  • \(\mathcal{M}_{\theta}:\underbrace{\mathbf{S}_{t-4:t}}_{\text{5 previous states}} \mapsto \underbrace{\mathbf{S}_{t+1:t+5}}_{\text{5 future states}}\)
  • Space-then-time attention: First attends to spatial features (Swin) then temporal dependencies.
  • Efficient: Reduces computational complexity while maintaining performance compared to all-at-once attention.*
* Source: Liu et al. (2025), BCAT: A Block Causal Transformer for PDE Foundation Models for Fluid Dynamics.
