Results

Conclusions

On the majority of datasets:
  • RQ1: Single-step accuracy flow matching outperforms the deterministic baseline
  • RQ2: Rollout stability flow matching models accumulate error more slowly over long horizons
  • RQ3: Spectral consistency flow matching reproduces energy and enstrophy spectra more accurately
  • Summary learning a distribution over future states yields a significant advantage, though it depends on the prior matching the data's frequency content
