Results

Conclusions

  • RQ1: Single-step accuracy flow matching outperforms the deterministic baseline on nearly all datasets
  • RQ2: Rollout stability probabilistic models accumulate error more slowly over long horizons
  • RQ3: Spectral consistency flow matching reproduces energy and enstrophy spectra more accurately
  • Summary learning a distribution over future states drives the advantage, though it depends on the prior matching the data's frequency content
  • Limitations
    • Prior definition a fixed, non-adapted prior limits performance on high-frequency, sharply-varying fields
    • Limitation: unobserved forcing no model receives external body-forcing information, capping rollout accuracy where such forcing is active
    • Limitation: comparability of models shared architecture aids fairness, but each formulation has its own tuning space, leaving part of the gap unexplained
    • Limitation: compressible spectral diagnostics velocity-only predictions can't yield energy or enstrophy spectra for the compressible dataset
    • Future work
      • data-dependent priors
      • forcing terms as symbolic input
      • hyperparameter tuning
      • discretized grid & obstructed flow & density input & pressure input
      • validation/fine-tuning to new flow regimes
